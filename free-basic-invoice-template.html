Basic Invoicing for Small Businesses and Solo Traders

A basic invoice covers essential transaction details without extra software complexity. It provides a clean, printable record for your bookkeeping and gives clients clear payment instructions.

Standard Invoice Elements

  • Company name, address, and email or phone
  • Client billing name and contact person
  • Invoice number, issue date, and payment due date
  • List of products delivered or hours worked with rates
  • Applicable sales tax or VAT, subtotal, and total due
  • Direct payment details (bank wire, check, or online payment)
