Essential Checklist for a Freelance Invoice

To avoid client disputes and payment delays, ensure your freelance invoice includes these 7 elements:

1. Contact & Studio Branding

Your legal name or DBA, business address, email, phone, and studio logo.

2. Unique Invoice Number

A sequential numbering system (e.g. INV-2026-001) for bookkeeping and accounting tracking.

3. Client Billing Details

Company name, accounts payable contact person, and billing address.

4. Itemized Deliverables

Clear description of work (e.g. "React Web App Frontend Sprint 1", "UI Design System", "Security Audit").

5. Hourly Rates or Milestone Pricing

Quantity, unit of measurement (hours, milestones, days), and unit rate.

6. Clear Payment Terms & Due Date

Specify Net 7, Net 14, or Net 30 payment terms and late payment terms.

7. Direct Payment Instructions

Bank wire details (IBAN / SWIFT / Routing / IFSC) or instant UPI QR code scan-and-pay.

When should I send my freelance invoice?
