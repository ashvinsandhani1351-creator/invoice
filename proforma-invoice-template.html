What is a Proforma Invoice Template?

A Proforma Invoice Template is an itemized commercial billing document issued to request payment for goods sold or services provided. It establishes a legally recognized payment obligation between the business and client.

Key Information to Include

  • Business Identification: Business name, logo, physical address, and registration number.
  • Client Details: Client name, company name, billing address, and contact details.
  • Sequential Numbering: Unique invoice number for accounting and tax reconciliation.
  • Itemized Line Items: Detailed list of products or services with unit rates, quantities, and line totals.
  • Tax Calculation & Banking: Applicable sales tax, GST, or VAT, and direct bank payment / QR code details.
